KDP Upload Settings Cheat-Sheet

Follow this step-by-step checklist when filling out the form on kdp.amazon.com.

1 Tab 1: Paperback Details
  • Book Title: Match the title of your project (My Jungle Coloring Book)
  • Subtitle: Paste subtitle generated in SEO tab
  • Author: Enter your Pen Name (Creative Kids Studio)
  • Description: Copy & paste HTML Description from Tab 3
  • Publishing Rights: Select "I own the copyright and hold necessary publishing rights"
  • Primary Audience: Sexually explicit images = No, Reading Age = 4-8 Years
  • Categories: Select the 3 categories suggested in Tab 2
  • Keywords: Paste the 7 keywords into slots 1 to 7
2 Tab 2: Paperback Content (Files & Print Options)
  • Print ISBN: Select "Assign me a free KDP ISBN"
  • Interior & Paper Type: Select "Black & white interior with white paper"
  • Trim Size: Select 8.5 x 11 in
  • Bleed Settings: Select "Bleed (PDF only)"
  • Cover Finish: Select "Matte" (smooth, premium feel, easy to hold)
  • Manuscript: Upload ...Interior_Print_Ready.pdf
  • Book Cover: Select "Upload a cover you already have" and upload ...Cover_Full_Wrap.pdf
  • Launch Previewer: Click and review; our PDFs pass with 0 errors!
3 Tab 3: Paperback Rights & Pricing
  • Territories: Select "All territories (worldwide rights)"
  • Primary Marketplace: Select "Amazon.com"
  • Amazon.com List Price: Enter $6.99 (or $7.99)
  • Click "Publish Your Paperback Book"! Your book will go live in 24-72 hours.
